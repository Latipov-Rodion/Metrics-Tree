> - **📊 Dashboard** — all your filled metrics on one page, color-coded

> - **🌐 3 languages** — English, Русский, Oʻzbekcha
> - **🔗 Clean per-metric URLs** — `metricstree.vercel.app/ltv`, `/burnMultiple`, `/runway`...
> - **♿ A11y, dark/light theme, keyboard shortcuts (press `?` for the cheat sheet)**
>
> **Free, no signup, no tracking.** Single-page static SPA, MIT license, [open-source on GitHub](https://github.com/Latipov-Rodion/Metrics-Tree).

## 3. Long description (PH "What is X?" field, ~500 chars)

> MetricTree is a free calculator for **47 product metrics** that PMs, marketers, finance leads, and founders use weekly: LTV, CAC, MRR, NRR, NPS, Burn Multiple, Runway, Sales Velocity, Rule of 40, and more.
>
> Each metric has a formula, industry benchmarks across 5 verticals, and a plain-language insight. **Goal mode** runs the formula in reverse — set a target, get the required input. **What-if sliders** let you drag any input in real time. **Embed mode** drops the calculator into your blog or Notion as an iframe.
>
> 3 languages (EN/RU/UZ), 4 currencies, dark mode, keyboard shortcuts, no signup.
