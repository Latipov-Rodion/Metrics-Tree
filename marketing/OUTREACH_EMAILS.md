## Email-tactics

1. **Personalize первое предложение** — read what they recently published, mention specific
2. **Don't ask for too much** — first contact = «hey here's a thing», not «please RT»
3. **Make their work easier** — embed-code ready, draft post ready, calc verified
4. **Follow up after 7 days** if no response — 1× polite bump
5. **Track in CSV** — name / email / sent date / status / response

Send 5 emails / day = 30 emails in 6 working days. Expected response rate: 15-30% = 5-9 replies = 3-5 backlinks/mentions.
